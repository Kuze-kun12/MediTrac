    MediTrac System Roles & Architecture Logic

    Plain-English breakdown of Admin, Pharmacist, and Health Worker responsibilities.

    👑 1. ADMINISTRATOR (Central Inventory & Boss) Primary Goal: Manages the master medicine catalog, oversees stock levels, and audits SQL join reports.

    • Adds, updates, and deletes medicines and safety reorder points.
    • Monitors system-wide automated alerts (Expired & Low Stock).
    • Runs join reports across all satellite health stations.
    💊 2. PHARMACIST (Dispensing to PATIENTS) Primary Goal: Clinic prescription fulfillment for individual patients.

    • Selects registered patients (e.g. Maria Santos, Juan Dela Cruz).
    • Allocates medicine batches using FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out).
    • Atomically deducts stock and maintains patient dispensing history.
    🏥 3. HEALTH WORKER (Distributing to EACH BARANGAY) Primary Goal: Community and satellite station medicine distribution.

    • Dispatches medicine batches to each Barangay Health Station (e.g. North Sub-Station, East Station, Valencia).
    • Inspects stock levels per barangay.
    • Receives safety warnings for satellite community stations.
